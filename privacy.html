Good Little Things

Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Good Little Things is a personal journal for remembering positive moments. We designed the app to keep your journal on your device rather than on servers operated by us. This Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app, contact us, or visit our legal pages.

1. Who we are

Good Little Things is provided by Anna Żurawska, based in Sweden (“we”, “us”, “our”). For privacy questions, contact goodlittlethings.app@gmail.com.

2. Data stored in the app

The app can store your journal entries and their dates and labels, photos you select, keepsakes and their appearance and personal notes, a name you choose for the greeting, and your preferences such as language, home style and reminder settings.

In this version, all of this is stored locally on your device. There is no Good Little Things account or sign-in, and the app does not send your journal, photos or name to us or to anyone else. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

What you write may be personal. Please only add content you are comfortable keeping on your device and sharing through any export or share feature you use.

3. Photos, reminders and dictation

Photos: when you choose to attach a photo, the system photo picker gives the app only the photos you select. The app keeps its own copy of each attached photo on your device so the memory keeps its picture. You can change photo access at any time in your device settings.

Reminders: if you turn on reminders, they are scheduled as local notifications on your device. No reminder content is sent through push services.

Dictation: the app does not record audio and does not ask for microphone access. If you use the dictation button on your iPhone keyboard, speech is handled by Apple under your Apple settings, not by the app.

4. Sharing, exports and backups

If you share a memory or a keepsake as a PDF through your device’s share menu, the file goes to the destination you choose and is then handled under that destination’s terms.

“Export data” in Settings → Data & Storage creates a backup file with your memories, keepsakes, settings and attached photos. The file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe. “Restore from backup” adds the memories and keepsakes from such a file back into the app.

Depending on your device settings, app data may also be included in Apple-managed device backups such as iCloud Backup. These are governed by your Apple settings and Apple’s terms. iCloud Backup is different from in-app synchronization: this version of Good Little Things does not offer its own iCloud synchronization.

5. Emails and support requests

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and any attachments you choose to send. Please do not send information you do not want us to receive. We use it to reply, investigate issues and, where appropriate, improve the app. We may keep a short, de-identified note of a reported problem without keeping the whole message.

Our legal basis for handling support emails is our legitimate interest in answering users and maintaining the app (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). You may object to this processing.

We use Gmail, provided by Google, to receive and manage emails, so Google processes email information as our service provider. This may involve access from outside the EEA, under Google’s safeguards for international transfers.

We delete support emails once the matter is resolved, and in any case no later than 12 months after it is resolved, unless a longer period is needed for a legal claim or obligation. You can ask us to delete them earlier.

6. App Store and our legal pages

Apple processes download and related information under its own privacy policy and your Apple settings. Apple may give us aggregated reports, such as download numbers and, if you have chosen to share them, crash reports. These do not include your journal content.

Our public Privacy Policy and Terms pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes; see GitHub’s privacy documentation.

7. Your choices and rights

You can edit or delete any memory or keepsake in the app, and delete everything with “Delete all data” in Settings → Data & Storage. This removes the app’s stored data and its copies of your photos from your device and cancels reminders. Deleting the app also removes its data from your device. Exported files and device backups are not affected and remain until you delete them.

Because we never receive your journal, we cannot view, retrieve or delete it for you. For personal data we handle through email, you may have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and, where applicable, portability. Contact us at goodlittlethings.app@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY) or another competent data protection authority.

8. Children

Good Little Things is intended for a general audience. It does not ask anyone, including children, for personal information, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when the app or the law changes, for example before adding optional iCloud synchronization or online features. We will update the date above and, where required, give notice before material changes apply.
